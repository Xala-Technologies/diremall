# Dire Mall brand kit

Files are in `public/brand/` (also served by the app at `/brand/...`).

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `dire-mall-mark.svg` | The mark alone, with the building and shops scene inside the D: app icon, stamps, social profile picture, anything 48 px or larger. |
| `dire-mall-mark-simple.svg` | The same D with plain gallery stripes, for tiny sizes (favicon, 16 to 30 px) where the scene would blur. |
| `dire-mall-mark-1024.png` | The same mark as a 1024 px image for places that do not take SVG. |
| `dire-mall-logo.svg` | Mark with the name, for light backgrounds (letterheads, signs, documents). |
| `dire-mall-logo-light.svg` | Mark with the name in white, for dark backgrounds. |

## The idea
A bold **D** for Dire. Its opening shows the mall itself, as seen from the courtyard looking up: the open galleries with
their lit shopfronts on both sides of the central stair core, and the little kiosk on the courtyard floor. The shop
signs pick up the colours of the real shopfronts (amber light, coral and blue signs).

## Colours
- Navy `#003057` (the mark's background, the name)
- White `#FFFFFF`
- Amber `#F5A623` and cream `#FFD58A` (lit shop windows), coral `#E4572E` and blue `#4DA3FF` (shop signs and awning), used only inside the scene
- Grey `#5B6B7C` (the "Dire Dawa · Ethiopia" line)

## Use
- Keep clear space around the mark of at least a quarter of its width. Do not stretch, recolour or add effects.
- Use the full mark from 30 px up and the simple one below that. In the app this switches automatically. Smallest for the name lockup: 120 px wide.
- The name lockup uses the Inter typeface as live text. For printing or sending to a sign maker, ask for the text to be
  converted to outlines first so it looks the same everywhere.
- This is a first design made from the building's character, not a registered trademark. Dire Mall should approve it
  before using it on signage or in print.
